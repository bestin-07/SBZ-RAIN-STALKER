Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short
  • No account, no cookies, no analytics, no ads.
  • Your location stays on your device. The app downloads the weather for all of Salzburg and picks your spot on the device – our server never receives your coordinates.
  • As with any website, the services that deliver the app (Cloudflare, Railway) and supply the map and radar images (Esri, RainViewer) see your IP address.
  • Only if you turn on notifications do we store your browser's push address – no name, email or location.

1. Controller

Bestin Antu, Salzburg, Austria
Email: contact@gemmaraus.at

Gemma Raus is a private, non-commercial project. No data protection officer is appointed (none is required).

2. Opening the app (hosting and delivery)

When you open the app, your IP address, the date and time, the requested address, your browser type and the referring page are necessarily processed. Involved are:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (San Francisco, USA) – routes all requests to our app, protects it from attacks and delivers files faster. If a request fails, your browser may send a technical error report to Cloudflare ("Network Error Logging").
  • Railway Corporation (San Francisco, USA) – runs our server and database.

Both act as our processors (Art. 28 GDPR). Our server limits how many requests one IP address may make per minute (abuse protection); that counter lives only in memory and expires after about a minute. Our database stores no IP addresses. Railway's request logs, which may contain IP addresses, are deleted after at most 30 days; Cloudflare keeps security and error logs only briefly.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in delivering the app securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers outside the EU: Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (European Commission adequacy decision); with Railway, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply (Decision 2021/914).

3. Your location

The app asks for your location only when you tap "Get my location", and only if you allow it in your browser. You can also use the app without it ("Use Salzburg center instead").

Your location is processed on your device: the app downloads the weather data for all of Salzburg from our server (the same for every user) and picks the point closest to you. Our server does not receive your coordinates.

Exception: if our server is unreachable, the app asks the weather services directly – for that request GeoSphere Austria (Vienna) and Open-Meteo (Switzerland) receive your coordinates and IP address (see section 4).

So the app can show weather instantly next time, your last location stays stored in your browser (section 6). You can withdraw location access at any time in your browser or phone settings.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in showing you the weather for the place you ask about (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Push notifications

Only if you tap the bell and agree in your browser. We then store in our database (at Railway):

  • the push address your browser creates for this, and two keys used to encrypt the messages,
  • a random ID that lets only your browser delete the subscription again,
  • the time you last opened the app.

No name, no email, no location. The alerts cover all of Salzburg and are the same for everyone. The push address is a pseudonymous identifier: we cannot link it to a person.

Messages are delivered by your browser's push service – Google (Chrome, Android), Mozilla (Firefox) or Apple (Safari, iPhone), all based in the USA. Your browser chooses the service, not us. The content is end-to-end encrypted; the push service cannot read it. Google and Apple are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Deletion: immediately when you switch the bell off; automatically if you don't open the app for 90 days or the push service reports that the subscription is no longer valid.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. In Austria you can consent yourself from the age of 14 (§ 4(4) DSG).

6. Storage on your device

The app sets no cookies. It stores what it needs to work in your browser's storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device:

  • theme and language (theme, lang),
  • your last location with its time (last_location) and a short weather history for that place (story), so the app says the same thing when you quickly reopen it,
  • a random number that varies the wording (phrase_seed),
  • the ID for unsubscribing from push (push_unsub_token),
  • what you have already seen or closed: warnings, install hint, release note, map hint (dismissed_warnings, install_prompt_seen, update_note_…, map_popup_seen).

On top of that come the app files in the offline cache (service worker) and short-lived entries for the update check (sessionStorage). You can remove all of it by clearing the site data for gemmaraus.at in your browser or uninstalling the app.

This storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for and therefore needs no consent (§ 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021).

7. Donations

The donate button leads to PayPal (PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A., Luxembourg). You only enter data there; PayPal's privacy policy applies. We see what PayPal shows recipients (usually name, email address and amount) and keep it only as long as legally required.

8. Contact by email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them once the matter is closed. Emails to contact@gemmaraus.at are forwarded by Cloudflare (Email Routing) to our mailbox at Google (Gmail). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

9. What we don't do

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics or advertising services, no cross-site tracking.
  • No profiling and no automated decisions about you (Art. 22 GDPR).
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • Our server stores weather data for 11 fixed points in the city – never your location.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 15–20 GDPR), and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not make earlier processing unlawful.

Because we store nothing that identifies you by name, we can only find your push subscription if you give us its push address (Art. 11 GDPR). The easiest way: switch the bell off – the subscription is deleted immediately. For anything else: contact@gemmaraus.at.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.

11. Changes

When the app changes, we update this policy. The version published here applies.
